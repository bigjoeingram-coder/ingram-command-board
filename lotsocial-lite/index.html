Correct product flow

The salesperson should do almost nothing

This page currently does not extract vehicle data or make video. That means it is not valid for reputation-bearing tester outreach yet.

1

Paste VDP URL

Salesperson pastes a live vehicle detail page from the dealership website.

2

Auto-pull vehicle facts

System extracts year, make, model, trim, price, mileage, photos, and visible equipment. No invented claims.

3

Generate caption options

Primary social caption, short caption, and text-message version are generated from source facts.

4

Create simple video

Use pulled vehicle photos to create a short inventory video or reel-style asset with caption overlay.

5

Review and copy/share

Salesperson reviews, makes small edits, then copies, downloads, or shares. No direct posting in MVP.
